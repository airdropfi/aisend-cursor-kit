# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `aisend` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. Anything that sends mail stops for your review first.

1. `Which sending domains are verified on my AISend account?`
2. `Draft a short email to me@example.com saying the deploy finished. Show me the full message and don't send anything until I say yes.`
3. `Show my 10 most recent AISend emails with their delivery status.`
4. `Did the email I just sent get delivered? Check its status.`
5. `Is someone@example.com a valid address to send to? Don't send anything.`
6. `List the emails that bounced or failed this week and tell me which recipients they went to.`
